Datasets & Medical Provenance

Standard medical benchmark datasets from the UCI Machine Learning Repository

Cardiology

Heart Disease Dataset

From the Cleveland Clinic Foundation (Detrano et al.). Predicts presence of coronary artery stenosis from 13 clinical biomarkers.

  • Records: 303 patients
  • Features: Age, Gender, Blood Pressure, Cholesterol, ECG, Max Heart Rate, etc.
  • Target: Disease Present (139) vs Normal (164)
Endocrinology

Pima Indians Diabetes

From the National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases (NIDDK). Predicts onset of diabetes based on diagnostic test measures.

  • Records: 768 patients
  • Features: Glucose, Blood Pressure, Insulin, BMI, Age, Pregnancies, etc.
  • Target: Diabetic (268) vs Non-diabetic (500)
Oncology

Breast Cancer Wisconsin (Diagnostic)

From the University of Wisconsin Hospitals (Wolberg, Street, Mangasarian). Predicts whether a tumor biopsy is Benign or Malignant.

  • Records: 569 cases
  • Features: Mean Radius, Texture, Perimeter, Area, Smoothness, Concavity, etc.
  • Target: Malignant (212) vs Benign (357)
